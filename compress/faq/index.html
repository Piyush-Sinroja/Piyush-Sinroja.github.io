Frequently asked questions

How Compress Images & Videos works, and answers to common questions. To contact us, open the app and tap Settings → Contact support.

How do I get started?

1. On the home screen, pick a task: compress photos or videos, convert images, or create a PDF.
2. Select photos or videos and tap Next.
3. Choose how small; the estimate shows the result before you start.
4. Tap Compress, then Save or Share. Your originals are never changed.

How do I compress photos?

Tap “Select Photos to Compress”, pick photos and tap Next.
Choose how small: Best quality, Balanced or Smallest.
Before you start, the estimate shows the expected size of the whole batch, and tells you if some photos would get bigger (often screenshots).
Tap Compress, then Save.

How do I make a photo an exact size in KB or MB for a form?

Choose “Exact file size” and pick a limit such as Under 50 KB, or type your own.
“Common uses” sets both the size and the pixels for exam photos, signatures, passport/visa photos and more.

How do I crop, rotate or resize a photo to exact pixels?

Before compressing, tap ✏️ next to a photo.
Drag the corners to crop, or pick a shape such as 1:1 or Passport 35×45.
Use Exact size for forms that need, for example, 200 × 230 px.
Your original photo is never changed.

How do I convert photos to JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC?

Tap “Convert Images” on the home screen to turn any photo into JPG or PNG (free), or WebP or HEIC (Pro).
Photos keep their full size and quality, so the files can get bigger — that’s normal.

How do I make a PDF from photos?

On Home, tap “Create PDF” and pick photos (free: up to 5, Pro: up to 100).
Choose the page size, photos per page (1, 2, 4 or 6), margins, and “Whole photo” or “Fill (crop)”.
Drag photos to change the order; tap the pencil to crop or rotate one.
“PDF size” can keep it under a limit, such as 1 MB for forms.
Add a password if you like, then share, save to Files or print.
Your layout is remembered for next time.

How do I compress videos?

Tap “Select Videos to Compress”.
720p (Balanced) is ideal for WhatsApp; “Exact file size” fits limits such as 16 MB or 25 MB.
After compressing, tap a video to play it and switch between Compressed and Original at the same moment.
For many videos, tap “Tips for big batches”.
Videos stored only in iCloud download while it compresses.

How are videos stored in iCloud handled?

Videos stored only in iCloud download one by one while compressing, two at a time.
Wi-Fi makes it faster. The Lock Screen shows “iCloud 74%” while one downloads.
If iCloud is slow or a video doesn't download, Compress tries again, goes on with the next videos and retries it at the end.

How do I save or share the results?

Save puts the results in the “Compress” album (or the original album — see Settings).
Share sends the files or one ZIP.
To save or share just one, tap it; to pick several, long-press one or tap “Select all”.
Results you haven’t saved are removed when you leave the screen — the app asks first.
Saved copies are marked “Compressed” in the photo picker.

How do I free up space on my phone?

After saving, choose “Delete originals” to remove the large originals.
Your phone asks once to confirm, and they stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
Turn on “Delete originals after saving” in Settings to skip our question.
The home screen shows how much space you’ve freed.

Are the date, camera, location and album kept?

Compressed photos keep the original date, camera, lens and location by default (WebP keeps none of it inside the file).
Compressed videos keep the date and location.
In Settings → Photo info you can remove the location or all info.
“Save to original album” keeps results next to the originals.

How do I work with big batches?

With many photos the list switches to a grid — use the grid/list button to change it.
Tap a photo for details, Compare, Save and Share (Edit is available before compressing).
Filters such as “Kept” or “Failed” and sorting help you find photos quickly.
Up to 3 photos are compressed at the same time and the screen stays on.
Long-press a photo to select several, then save, share or remove them together.

What can I do with Advanced (Pro)?

Choose “Advanced” for full control: quality or “Max file size” (any format), size including “Exact size W × H…” (e.g. 1080 × 1080 with Fit, Fill or Pad), and format.
Under “Extras”: “File names” (e.g. Shop_001), DPI for printing and forms, black & white, and a “Watermark” with a live preview on your own photo.

How do Pro, subscriptions and restore work?

Monthly and Lifetime unlock the same Pro.
Settings → “Manage subscription” cancels or changes Monthly; Pro stays until the paid month ends.
Settings → “Switch to Lifetime” pays once instead; then cancel Monthly so you aren't charged again.
On a new phone, Settings → “Restore purchases” brings Pro back with the same store account.

What can I change in Settings?

Tap ⚙️ on the home screen.
“Appearance”: “System default”, “Light” or “Dark”.
“Language” changes the app language.
“Photo info”, “Save to original album” and “Transparent areas” decide how results are saved.
On a new phone, “Restore purchases” brings Pro back with the same store account.
If you pay monthly, “Manage subscription” cancels or changes it, and “Switch to Lifetime” lets you pay once instead.

How do I contact you?

Many answers are in “Help & FAQ”.
To write to us, open Settings → “Contact support”, pick a topic and type your message.
Add your email so we can reply; to show a screenshot, attach it when you answer our email.
App and device details help us fix problems faster; no photos or files are sent.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your phone.

Why was my photo kept unchanged?

If a photo is already smaller than your choice, we keep the original instead of making it bigger.

Where are compressed photos saved?

In the “Compress” album, or in the original album if you turn that on in Settings.

Can I get deleted originals back?

Yes. Deleted originals stay in Recently Deleted (or Trash) for 30 days.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, using the same store account.

Why did my file get bigger after converting?

Converting keeps full size and quality. Some formats are larger, for example HEIC → JPG or a screenshot saved as JPG. To make files smaller, use “Select Photos to Compress” instead.

How many photos can I use at once?

Free: 5 photos or 1 video at a time, and 5 photos per PDF. Pro: up to 500 photos, 100 videos and 100 photos per PDF.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Compressing, converting and creating PDFs work without internet. Only purchases, ads and Contact support need a connection.

Can I use other apps while it compresses?

Yes. It's fastest with Compress open, but it keeps working in the background and lets you know when it's done. If your phone pauses it, open Compress and it continues; finished results are kept.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

In Settings, tap “Manage subscription”. Pro stays until the end of the month you paid for. To pay once instead, tap “Switch to Lifetime”, then cancel the Monthly subscription so you aren't charged again.

Which photo and video formats are supported?

Photos: JPG, HEIC, PNG and WebP. Videos: MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP; results are saved as MP4, which plays everywhere. More video formats are planned.

Why do iCloud videos take longer?

They are downloaded from iCloud first, one by one while compressing. The speed depends on your internet and on iCloud; Wi-Fi is faster. If one doesn't download, Compress goes on and tries it again at the end.

I got “Compressing stopped”. What now?

Your phone ended the work in the background, for example to save memory. Finished results are kept. Open Compress and tap Compress to do the rest.

Can I compress videos from WhatsApp, Files or other apps?

Yes. In that app, tap Share and choose Compress. Supported files open in Compress; files in other formats are left out.

What data does the app collect?

Your photos and videos never leave your phone. The app uses anonymous usage statistics (for example, which features are used) to improve it, and the free version shows ads from Google. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Why does a video say “Couldn't download it from iCloud”?

iCloud didn't deliver it, often because of a weak connection or a busy iCloud. Check your internet, then add the video again and compress it.
