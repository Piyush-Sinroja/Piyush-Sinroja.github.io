Privacy Policy — Compress Images & Videos

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the app Compress Images & Videos ("Compress", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information on iPhone, iPad and Android.

In short: your photos, videos and PDFs are processed only on your device. We never upload, collect or see them. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Purchases are handled by Apple, Google and RevenueCat. We use Google Firebase to count which features are used and to receive crash reports, never your photos or file names. If you contact support, we receive your message, your email and basic app and device details so we can reply.

1. Who we are

Compress is published by Piyush Sinroja.
Contact: appcoderhelp@gmail.com

2. Your photos, videos and PDFs

  • To compress, resize, convert or edit photos and videos, or to make a PDF from photos, the app asks for permission to access your photo library. You can allow all photos, only selected photos, or nothing, and you can change this at any time in your device's Settings.
  • All processing happens on your device. Your photos, videos and PDFs are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them.
  • Compressed copies are saved to your photo library (in a "Compress" album) only when you tap Save. PDFs are only shared, saved or printed when you choose to.
  • While the app is working, it keeps temporary copies in the app's private storage. These are removed when you start a new batch, clear the list, or the next time the app starts.
  • If you choose to delete originals after saving, the app moves them to "Recently Deleted" (iPhone) or the trash (Android), where your device asks you to confirm.
  • Photos or videos you send to the app from other apps (the Share menu) are handled in the same way: on your device only. The copies the system hands to the app are removed after one day.
  • A PDF password you set is used only to protect that PDF on your device. It is never saved or sent anywhere.

3. Location and other information inside your photos

Photos and videos can contain information such as the date taken and the place (GPS location) where they were taken. Depending on the setting you choose in the app, this information is kept or removed in the compressed copies.

The app never asks for, reads or tracks your current location. It only copies the location that is already stored in your own photos, and only on your device.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), only if you allow it;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device type, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps), and diagnostic data.

Your choices:

  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not used, and ads are not personalised with it.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: we ask for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Pro removes all ads.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. App usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand which features are used and to fix crashes, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) record events such as "photos compressed", "PDF created", "paywall shown" or "purchase completed", with numbers and options only: for example how many photos, the percentage saved, the compression mode, or the number of PDF pages. They also record which screens are opened, which settings are changed (the choice only, such as "Dark"), which help topics are read, and whether you have Pro. Firebase adds standard information such as an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, app version, language, country (derived from your IP address) and session times.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are sent when the app crashes or hits a serious error. They contain technical details: where in the app the problem happened and the error message, device model, operating system, app version, free memory and storage, and a random installation identifier. In rare cases an error message can include the name of a temporary file inside the app's private storage.

What Firebase never receives from us: your photos, videos, PDFs, their contents, the location inside your photos, your email, or your advertising identifier. Usage statistics never include file names. Firebase is not used for advertising, and we turned off the collection of advertising identifiers in Firebase.

Statistics and crash reports are kept by Google for a limited time: about 2 months for detailed usage events and 90 days for crash reports.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Contacting support

When you send a message with Settings → Contact support in the app, or with our online support form, we receive:

  • the topic you chose and your message;
  • the email address you enter, so we can reply;
  • app and device details, which are added automatically and shown to you before sending ("See what's sent"): app version, phone or tablet model, operating-system version, app language, appearance (light or dark), whether you have Pro, free storage, and screen size.

No photos, videos or files are sent. Messages are stored in Google Forms in our own Google account. We use them only to answer you and to fix problems, never for marketing. We delete them when they are no longer needed, and you can ask us to delete yours at any time.

7. Purchases (Pro)

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card details.

To check whether you have Pro and to let you restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID created by the app, your purchase receipts and history, device and operating-system information, and your IP address. We do not create accounts and do not ask for your name or email to make a purchase.

RevenueCat's Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

8. Information stored on your device

The app stores your settings (for example language, appearance, quality, PDF layout and metadata choices) and simple statistics such as how much space you have saved. This information stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not upload your photos, videos, PDFs or their contents.
  • We do not use usage statistics, crash reports or support messages for advertising.

10. Ratings and reviews

The app may ask you to rate it using the system's built-in review prompt. This is handled entirely by Apple or Google.

11. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to object to personalised advertising. This includes rights under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws.

Because we do not collect your photos, videos or account details, most information exists only on your device, and you control it directly by deleting it or uninstalling the app. To access or delete a support message you sent us, contact us. For information held by Google (AdMob and Firebase) or RevenueCat, you can use their tools listed above, or contact us and we will help.

California residents: using an advertising identifier for personalised ads may be considered "sharing" under California law. You can opt out by choosing "Ask App Not to Track" on iPhone and iPad, by deleting your Advertising ID on Android, or by upgrading to Pro.

Grievance contact (India): appcoderhelp@gmail.com

13. Security

Your photos, videos and PDFs never leave your device through the app. Communication with Google (AdMob, Firebase and Google Forms) and RevenueCat uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. For important changes, we will also let you know in the app.

15. Contact
