Features

Compress photos

Best quality, Balanced or Smallest: usually 60–90% smaller with the same look. HEIC, JPG, PNG and WebP. Compare any result with its original.

Exact size in KB or MB

Under 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB, 1 MB… or your own size. Ready-made settings for exam photos, signatures, passport/visa and profile photos.

Crop & resize

Crop, rotate, flip or black & white. Passport/visa frames (35 × 45 mm, US 2 × 2 in) and exact pixel sizes such as 200 × 230.

Convert photos

HEIC to JPG or PNG so they open everywhere. WebP and HEIC with Pro. Many photos at once, full size and high quality.

Photos to PDF

A4, US Letter, Legal and more. 1, 2, 4 or 6 photos per page, a size limit such as 1 MB, page numbers or a password. Share, save or print.

Compress videos

1080p, 720p (great for WhatsApp) or 480p, or an exact size such as under 16 MB. Play and compare before and after.
