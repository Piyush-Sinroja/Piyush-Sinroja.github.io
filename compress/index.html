Features

Everything you need to shrink files

From one photo for a visa form to hundreds of videos taking up space.

Compress photos

Best quality, Balanced or Smallest: usually 60–90% smaller with the same look. HEIC, JPG, PNG and WebP. Compare any result with its original.

Exact size in KB or MB

Under 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB, 1 MB… or your own size. Ready-made settings for exam photos, signatures, passport/visa and profile photos.

Crop & resize

Crop, rotate, flip or black & white. Passport/visa frames (35 × 45 mm, US 2 × 2 in) and exact pixel sizes such as 200 × 230.

Convert photos

HEIC to JPG or PNG so they open everywhere. WebP and HEIC with Pro. Many photos at once, full size and high quality.

Photos to PDF

A4, US Letter, Legal and more. 1, 2, 4 or 6 photos per page, a size limit such as 1 MB, page numbers or a password. Share, save or print.

Compress videos

1080p, 720p (great for WhatsApp) or 480p, or an exact size such as under 16 MB. MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP; saved as MP4.

Privacy

Everything stays on your phone

Your photos and videos are never sent anywhere. Not to us, not to anyone.

Nothing is uploaded

All work happens on your iPhone or iPad. Works offline, even in airplane mode.

Your originals are safe

Compress always makes a new copy. Deleting big originals is optional, and they stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

No account needed

No sign-up, no email, no login. Open the app and start.
