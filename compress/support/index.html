Support — Compress Images & Videos

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Contact us

  • In the app (fastest): open Settings → Contact support, choose a topic, write your message and enter your email. Your app version and phone model are added automatically, so we can help without asking back.
  • Online: use our support form: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdCANPekjv4lBO-cVvwvALpQeo-dooenSEujC1sWy_xOne0IA/viewform

We reply by email, usually within 7–10 working days. Need to show a screenshot? Just attach it when you reply to our email.

If you use the online form, please include your phone or tablet model, iOS or Android version, and the app version (Settings → About in the app).

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your device. Your photos, videos and PDFs are never uploaded, and we cannot see them. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I make a photo smaller than a certain size (for example under 50 KB for a form)?

Choose Exact file size, pick or type the size (for example 50 KB), and tap Compress. For forms that also need exact pixels (for example 200 × 230), use the ready-made form sizes or Edit → exact size.

How do I convert photos to JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC?

On the home screen, tap Convert Images, select your photos, choose the format, and tap Convert. Any photo works, not only HEIC.

Which photo and video formats are supported?

Photos: JPG, HEIC, PNG and WebP. Results can be saved as JPG or PNG, and as WebP or HEIC with Pro.
Videos: MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP. Results are saved as MP4, which plays everywhere. More video formats, such as MKV and AVI, are planned for a future update.

How do I make a PDF from photos?

On the home screen, tap Create PDF and select your photos. Choose the page size (A4, US Letter, Fit to photo, or More sizes such as A3, A5, Legal or photo sizes), orientation, photos per page, margins and PDF size. You can drag photos to change the order, add page numbers or a password, then Share, Save or Print.

Where are my compressed photos and videos saved?

In your Photos app, in the album Compress (or in the original album, if you turned that on in Settings). Nothing is saved until you tap Save.

Will my originals be deleted?

Only if you choose to. After saving, the app can offer to delete the originals to free up space. They go to Recently Deleted (iPhone and iPad) or the trash (Android), so you can still recover them for a while.

Why is a result bigger than the original, or "kept original"?

Some photos are already highly compressed. If compressing would not make a photo smaller, the app keeps your original instead of making a bigger copy.

The date or location of my photos changed or disappeared.

In the app's Settings → Photo info, choose Keep all (date, camera, location), Remove location, or Remove all.

Video compression is slow or stops.

Long or 4K videos take time. Keep the app open: the screen stays on while it works. Make sure there is enough free storage, at least the size of the video. If storage gets too low, the app pauses safely, keeps everything already finished, and you can continue after freeing space. If it still fails, contact us with the video's length and resolution.

How many photos or videos can I do at once?

Free: 5 photos, 1 video and 5 photos per PDF at a time. Pro: up to 500 photos, 100 videos and 100 photos per PDF. Very large photos (about 24 megapixels and up) are processed one at a time so the app stays smooth.

Can I use dark mode?

Yes. Open Settings → Appearance and choose System default, Light or Dark.

The app can't see my photos.

Open your device's Settings → Compress → Photos and choose Full Access (or add more photos if you chose "Limited Access").

Pro, purchases and subscriptions

What does Pro include?

No ads, bigger batches (up to 500 photos, 100 videos and 100 photos per PDF), Advanced settings (exact quality, size and format, watermark, file names, DPI and more), and all output formats. Open Go Pro in the app to see the full comparison.

I bought Pro but still see ads / Pro is not active.

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the app, using the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

  • In the app: Settings → Manage subscription.
  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Compress → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Compress → Cancel.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. After cancelling, Pro stays until the end of the month you paid for.

I pay monthly. Can I switch to Lifetime?

Yes. In the app, open Settings → Switch to Lifetime and buy Lifetime. Then cancel the Monthly subscription (Settings → Manage subscription), otherwise Apple or Google keeps charging it. The app reminds you after the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by us:

  • iPhone and iPad: https://reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Android: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637
